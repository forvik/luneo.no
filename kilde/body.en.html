Why Luneo

We have worked the front desk ourselves.

We have run activity centres in Norway for more than ten years. We have used the usual booking systems, and we know where they fall short. That is why we also know what a good booking system for this industry needs to include.

One booking occupies several resources at once

Eight people bowling means two lanes. A birthday package is a lane from 16:00 and a room from 17:00. Ordinary booking systems cannot handle that. Luneo assigns the resources itself – lanes, rooms, karts, tables – holds them, and releases them if the booking falls through.

You already have a website you like

Booking should plug into it, not replace it. Luneo lives on your own address, in your colours and under your name. Nothing the guest sees carries our name.

VAT has to be right all the way through

Every activity has its own rate, and in a package the food share is 25 % while the rest follows the activity. It carries unchanged from the price list to the payment window to the accounting report.

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 September 2026

Who we are

Luneo (luneo.no) is operated by Digikrutt.no, c/o Andreas Forvik, post@digikrutt.no. This policy explains what data we process, why, and what rights you have.

Two roles

Luneo is used by activity centres (our customers) to take bookings from their guests. That gives two roles:

  • For guests who book: The activity centre you book with is the data controller for your data. Luneo is the data processor and processes the data on behalf of the centre under a data processing agreement. If you have questions about your booking, contact the centre.
  • For our customers and visitors to luneo.no: Digikrutt.no is the data controller.

What data is processed

  • When booking: name, e-mail address, phone number, what you booked (time, activity, group size, extras) and anything you write in the form yourself. Payment details are processed by the payment provider (Dintero) – Luneo does not store card numbers.
  • For gift cards, loyalty club, newsletter and feedback: the data needed for the feature, for example e-mail address and points balance. Newsletters are only sent to those who have consented.
  • For our customers’ staff: name, account and a log of changes made in the system.
  • Technical: necessary operational logs (timestamps, IP address, error messages) to keep the service secure and stable.

What the data is used for

To carry out and manage the booking, send the confirmation and link to your booking, let you change or cancel, handle payment, gift cards and the loyalty club, and give the activity centre reports on its own operations. The legal basis is the contract with you (the booking), consent where obtained (newsletter), and legitimate interest in secure operations.

Retention and deletion

Old bookings are archived and deleted automatically after retention periods the activity centre sets in the system. Accounting data is kept as long as the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act requires. Newsletter data is deleted when you unsubscribe.

Who the data is shared with

We never sell data. We use sub-processors for hosting (cloud storage), sending e-mail and payment (Dintero, which in turn uses Vipps, card networks, Klarna and others). Sub-processors only process data under agreement with us.

Cookies and tracking

luneo.no uses no tracking cookies. The booking form embedded on customers’ websites contains no tracking and no marketing cookies. The booking pages use only what is technically necessary for the booking to work.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to the processing. Consent can be withdrawn at any time. For a booking, contact the activity centre. For luneo.no or the customer relationship, write to post@digikrutt.no. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest change.

Terms of purchase

Last updated 12 September 2026 · Applies to business customers

1. The agreement

These terms apply between Digikrutt.no, c/o Andreas Forvik (“Luneo”, “we”) and the business that adopts the Luneo booking system (“the customer”, “you”). The agreement is entered into when we set up the business in the system and the customer is given access.

2. Trial period

All customers may try Luneo free of charge for three months from the date access is given. During the trial the system can be set up and tried without being put into use. The trial ends at no cost if the customer does not wish to continue.

3. Prices

  • The booking itself costs €75 per month ex. VAT. We take no share of the customer’s revenue.
  • Add-on features are bought one at a time in the back office, at the monthly price shown there (ex. VAT) when the feature is switched on.
  • When a feature is switched on, the price and the terms text in force that day are stored. These are not changed for the customer without it being visible in the system.
  • Payments from guests go through the customer’s own agreement with the payment provider (Dintero). Transaction fees follow that agreement and come in addition to Luneo’s monthly price.

4. Invoicing

The monthly price for booking and active add-ons is invoiced monthly after the trial period. Payment is due 14 days from the invoice date. Invoices are sent from our accounting system.

5. Cancellation

There is no lock-in period. An add-on is cancelled with one button in the back office; it runs to the end of the current month and is not invoiced further. The whole agreement is cancelled in writing to post@digikrutt.no and runs to the end of the current month. On cancellation the customer may have their data exported, after which the data is deleted in line with the privacy policy.

6. The customer’s responsibilities

The customer is the data controller for its guests’ data and sets its own retention periods, prices, opening hours, guest terms and content in the system. The customer keeps its credentials (staff accounts and PINs) confidential. Only websites the customer has registered may embed the booking.

7. Operation, availability and support

Luneo is delivered as a web-based service with no installation. We work for high uptime and give notice of planned maintenance where possible. Setup and development mainly take place in the evenings and at weekends. Enquiries go to post@digikrutt.no.

8. Liability

We are not liable for indirect losses, such as lost revenue, resulting from downtime or errors. Our total liability is limited to what the customer has paid for the service over the past twelve months. The limitation does not apply in cases of gross negligence or intent.

9. Changes

We may change the terms and prices for new purchases with reasonable notice. Prices and terms for features the customer has already switched on are not changed without it being visible in the system, cf. section 3.

10. Governing law

The agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes are resolved first by a joint attempt at an amicable solution, and thereafter by the ordinary courts with Oslo District Court as the legal venue.
