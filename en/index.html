Why Luneo

We have worked the front desk ourselves.

We have run activity centres in Norway for more than ten years. We have used the usual booking systems, and we know where they fall short. That is why we also know what a good booking system for this industry needs to include.

One booking occupies several resources at once

Eight people bowling means two lanes. A birthday package is a lane from 16:00 and a room from 17:00. Ordinary booking systems cannot handle that. Luneo assigns the resources itself – lanes, rooms, karts, tables – holds them, and releases them if the booking falls through.

You already have a website you like

Booking should plug into it, not replace it. Luneo lives on your own address, in your colours and under your name. Nothing the guest sees carries our name.

VAT has to be right all the way through

Every activity has its own rate, and in a package the food share is 25 % while the rest follows the activity. It carries unchanged from the price list to the payment window to the accounting report.

What you get

Your own booking system, on your own address.

You get booking.yourdomain.com or name.luneo.no – with your own name, logo, colours and wording. That is not a setting; it is how the system is built. Three surfaces are included, in Norwegian and English.

For the guest

The booking pages

Where the guest books, changes and pays – on mobile or desktop. Every guest receives an e-mail confirmation with a link to their own booking.

  • Waiting list
  • “My booking”
For the staff

Back office

Everything the staff needs to run the day and the business. All of it can be changed by you.

  • Day plan

  • Reports
For the venue

The board

A screen showing who is playing on which lane right now, and who is up next. Just a browser on a TV – always available. The QR code for the waiting list can be switched on and off.

  • Lane overview
  • Up next
  • Waiting list (optional)

The guest manages their own booking

The e-mail confirmation contains a link to the booking. There the guest can change the number of people, add extra products, food and extras, or cancel – right up to the deadline you set, for example 48 hours before. That means fewer phone calls to the front desk.

Online payment is optional

You decide whether guests pay online. Switch it on and we connect Dintero, so guests pay the way they prefer – at transaction rates we have negotiated. Switch it off and you take payment at the front desk when the guest arrives, just as before. The booking works the same either way.

  • Vipps
  • Credit cards
  • Klarna
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • and more

Invoicing business customers

Business customers enter their invoicing details – company name, registration number, invoice address and reference – when they book. Everything sits on the booking itself, so you can invoice them easily afterwards.

Independently security tested

The system has been security tested by an independent third party. Everything is stored securely in the cloud with daily backups, so you do not lose bookings if something goes wrong.

Booking can be embedded straight into your website

Two lines pasted into your own website, and the form sits right inside your own page – in your colours, light or dark, without the guest ever leaving your site. No tracking, no cookies. Only websites you have registered yourself are allowed to embed the booking.

<script src="https://booking.yourdomain.com/embed.js"></script>
<div data-luneo="booking"></div>
